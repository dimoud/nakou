Services

Legal cover at every step

Full legal support when buying or selling property: title checks, registration with the Land Registry (Ktimatologio), preliminary and final contracts, and clearing liens and mortgages.

Drafting and legal review of sale, donation, parental gift and loan-for-use contracts, protecting your interests from the first draft to the signature.

Commercial agreements, residential and business leases, employment contracts. Written in clear terms, so you know what you are signing and what protects you.

Certified representation for main and supplementary pension applications with e-EFKA: insurance history review, recognition of insured periods and follow-through until the pension is awarded.
